Our Story

Founded in 2010 in the heart of India's chilli country — Guntur, Andhra Pradesh — RedSpice Trading Co. began with a simple mission: to bring the finest dry red chillies from Indian farms to kitchens and food businesses around the world.

What started as a small family operation has grown into one of the region's most trusted chilli trading companies, serving over 500 clients across 20+ countries. Our deep roots in the farming community give us unmatched access to premium-quality produce at competitive prices.

Why Choose Us

Direct Farm Sourcing

We work directly with a network of 200+ trusted farmers across Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, and Jammu & Kashmir. No middlemen means better quality and fairer prices for everyone.

Rigorous Quality Control

Every batch undergoes multi-stage inspection for moisture content, color value (ASTA rating), pungency (SHU), and contamination. We maintain FSSAI, ISO 22000, and HACCP certifications.

Modern Processing

Our state-of-the-art processing facility handles cleaning, sorting, de-stemming, grinding, and packaging under strict hygienic conditions with full traceability.

Flexible Solutions

From 1 kg retail packs to 20-ton container loads, we offer flexible packaging and order quantities. Private labeling and custom blends available on request.
