## About Mundu / Round Chilli

The Mundu chilli, also known as the Round or Gundu chilli, is a uniquely shaped variety popular in South Indian cooking. It is recognized for:

- **Spherical shape** unlike typical elongated chillies
- **High pungency** with intense, sharp heat
- **Compact size** (2-3 cm diameter)
- **Strong flavor** that holds up in pickles and preserves

## Applications

- Traditional South Indian pickles (achar)
- Chutneys and condiments
- Regional spice blends
- Specialty food products
- Dried whole chilli garnishing
