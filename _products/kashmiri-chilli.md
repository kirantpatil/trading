## About Kashmiri Chilli

The Kashmiri chilli is the mildest and most aromatic variety in our collection. Grown in the Kashmir Valley, it is treasured for:

- **Brilliant crimson color** that transforms any dish
- **Very mild heat** suitable for color-focused applications
- **Rich aroma** with fruity and slightly smoky notes
- **Premium positioning** in spice markets worldwide

## Applications

- Tandoori preparations (the classic red color)
- Rogan Josh and other Kashmiri dishes
- Premium spice blends and garam masala
- Marinades and rubs
- Gourmet food products
