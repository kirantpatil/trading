## About Guntur Sannam S4

The Guntur Sannam, classified as S4, is the most widely traded dry red chilli variety from India. Grown predominantly in the Guntur district of Andhra Pradesh, this chilli is prized for its:

- **Deep red color** with excellent ASTA color values (60-80)
- **Moderate to high pungency** making it versatile for many cuisines
- **Consistent quality** due to well-established cultivation practices
- **Excellent shelf life** when properly dried and stored

## Applications

- Chilli powder manufacturing
- Curry pastes and spice blends
- Food processing industry
- Oleoresin extraction
- Export markets (USA, UK, Southeast Asia, Middle East)
