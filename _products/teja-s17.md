## About Teja S17

The Teja chilli (S17 grade) is the hottest commonly traded Indian chilli variety. Cultivated mainly in Telangana and parts of Andhra Pradesh, it is valued for:

- **Very high pungency** making it the go-to for heat-intensive applications
- **Good red color** with reasonable ASTA values
- **Compact size** (5-7 cm) making it efficient for processing
- **Strong demand** in oleoresin and hot sauce industries

## Applications

- Oleoresin extraction (capsaicin-rich)
- Hot sauces and chilli pastes
- Spicy snack food seasoning
- Industrial food processing
- Pharmaceutical and defense applications (capsaicin)
