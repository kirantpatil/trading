## About Wrinkled Chilli 334

The 334 variety, commonly known as the Wrinkled Chilli, is a medium-heat chilli widely used in food processing. Key characteristics include:

- **Distinctive wrinkled texture** on the skin
- **Medium pungency** suitable for a wide range of applications
- **Good color retention** in processed products
- **Cost-effective** option for large-scale food manufacturing

## Applications

- Chilli flakes for pizza and seasoning
- Blended spice powders
- Ready-to-eat food manufacturing
- Pickle and sauce production
- Export markets for chilli flakes
