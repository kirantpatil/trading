## About Byadgi Chilli

The Byadgi chilli, named after the town of Byadgi in Karnataka's Haveri district, is one of India's most prized chilli varieties. It is distinguished by its:

- **Exceptionally deep red color** with very high ASTA values (100-180)
- **Mild pungency** making it ideal for color without excessive heat
- **Wrinkled, thin skin** that produces a rich, smooth powder
- **Distinctive flavor** with earthy, slightly sweet notes

## Applications

- Natural food coloring (preferred over synthetic alternatives)
- Premium curry powders and spice blends
- Tandoori and tikka marinades
- South Indian cuisine (Sambar, Rasam, chutneys)
- Oleoresin extraction for food industry
